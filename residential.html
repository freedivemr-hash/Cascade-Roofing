Residential Roofing Systems Engineered for Oregon Rain

Installed by our own hourly W-2 employees. As CertainTeed Select ShingleMasters—representing the top 1% of roofing contractors nationwide—we build lifetime roofing assemblies with guaranteed ventilation and non-prorated warranties.

The Complete Pacific Northwest Roofing Assembly

In the Pacific Northwest, installing shingles over an existing roof or skipping proper flashing guarantees premature moisture damage. A roof is not merely shingles; it is an integrated water-shedding system composed of decking inspection, moisture barriers, and attic ventilation.

1. Complete Tear-Off and Structural Deck Inspection

Every residential roof replacement begins with tearing off all existing layers down to the bare wood sheathing. Our craftsmen inspect every square foot of plywood or tongue-and-groove planking, identifying hidden dry rot caused by condensation or previous leaks. Any compromised wood or deteriorated barge boards are replaced before new underlayment is applied.

2. High-Performance Moisture Protection

  • Ice & Water Shield: Installed along all eaves, valleys, sidewalls, and around skylights to prevent wind-driven rain and ice dams from penetrating the decking.
  • Synthetic Breathable Underlayment: Commercial-grade synthetic felt that repels moisture while allowing internal vapor to escape, far outlasting traditional 15lb felt.
  • Heavy Gauge Metal Flashings: Step flashing at sidewalls, pre-formed valley metal, and corrosion-resistant lead plumbing pipe boot flashings.

3. Balanced Attic Ventilation Science

More Pacific Northwest roofs fail from interior attic condensation than exterior rainfall. When warm, moisture-laden air from living areas enters an unventilated attic, it condenses against cold roof decking, causing mold, deck rot, and shingle blistering. Cascade Roofing calculates intake (soffit) and exhaust (ridge vent) ratios on every home to ensure continuous airflow conforming to regional building codes.

4. CertainTeed Landmark & Landmark Pro Shingles

We recommend heavyweight dimensional fiberglass composite shingles featuring StreakFighter algae resistance and Class A fire ratings. Applied with a strict 6-nail high-wind fastening pattern, these systems withstand sustained 110–130 MPH gusts.

5. SureStart PLUS 50-Year Non-Prorated Warranty

Because Cascade Roofing is a factory-certified CertainTeed Select ShingleMaster, our customers qualify for the 50-Year SureStart PLUS warranty. This covers 100% of materials, labor, tear-off, and disposal fees with zero proration for up to 50 years, and is fully transferable to the next homeowner.
