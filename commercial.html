Commercial Flat & Low-Slope Roofing Systems

Engineered single-ply TPO, PVC, and modified bitumen installations for office parks, industrial complexes, and multi-family HOAs across Multnomah, Clackamas, and Washington counties.

Industrial Reliability for Portland Commercial Properties

Flat and low-slope roofs in the Pacific Northwest face relentless hydrostatic pressure from continuous rainfall and pine needle buildup. Water that ponds on a commercial roof for more than 48 hours breaks down adhesives, causes structural deflection, and risks interior inventory or tenant disruptions.

Thermoplastic Polyolefin (TPO) Systems

TPO is the premier single-ply membrane for Northwest commercial facilities. It combines the weather resistance of rubber with the heat-weldability of thermoplastic.

  • Hot-Air Welded Seams: Robotic and hand-welded seams fuse into a monolithic, vulcanized barrier 4x stronger than taped EPDM seams.
  • Energy-Star Reflectivity: High solar reflectance index (SRI) lowers interior cooling loads in summer and extends equipment lifespan.
  • Puncture & Chemical Resistance: Highly resistant to HVAC chemical condensation, grease exhaust, and foot traffic.

Custom Tapered Polyiso Insulation

To solve standing water, Cascade Roofing designs custom tapered polyisocyanurate (polyiso) insulation systems. By establishing positive slopes (minimum 1/4" per foot) toward interior roof drains and exterior scuppers, we eliminate ponding areas before new membrane application.

Preventative Maintenance & Commercial Service Contracts

Most manufacturer warranties require semi-annual documentation of roof drain clearing and membrane inspections. Our dedicated commercial maintenance division provides scheduled inspections, photographic condition logs, drain strainer clearing, and emergency response across the metro area.
