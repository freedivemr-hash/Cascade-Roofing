Over 65 Years of Dedicated Roofing in Portland

Founded in 1959, Cascade Roofing is a family-owned and operated contractor. We believe the only way to guarantee a dependable roof in the rainy Pacific Northwest is to directly employ and train our craftsmen.

Our Heritage: Rooted in Oregon Since 1959

When Cascade Roofing began operations in 1959, the Portland skyline looked very different, but our core mission has remained unchanged: provide homeowners and commercial building operators with reliable, transparent, and durable roofing solutions.

Operating out of our central facility on SW Hampton Street in Tigard, we have served multiple generations of Oregon families. In an industry where contractors frequently dissolve and reincorporate under new names every three to five years to shed warranty obligations, Cascade Roofing has maintained continuous, licensed, and bonded operations for more than six decades.

The Hourly Craftsman Difference: Zero Subcontractors

The standard business model across the roofing industry relies on piece-rate subcontracting. Under this model, third-party crews are paid a fixed dollar amount per "square" (100 square feet) of shingles installed. This structure creates an inherent conflict of interest: the faster the crew works, the more money they make, incentivizing installers to rush past hidden dry rot, skip step-flashing around chimneys, or skimp on nails.

Cascade Roofing rejects this model entirely. Every single installer on your roof is a full-time, hourly-paid W-2 employee of Cascade Roofing. Our team members receive:

  • Full healthcare and retirement benefit packages.
  • Comprehensive OSHA-certified safety and fall-protection training.
  • CertainTeed Master Shingle Applicator continuing factory credentials.

Because our craftsmen are paid hourly, they are instructed to prioritize quality over speed. If they find damaged wood decking or deteriorated barge boards, they take the time to repair them properly.

Environmental Stewardship: Over 90% Debris Recycled

Tear-off roofing debris represents one of the largest single contributors to construction waste in municipal landfills. At Cascade Roofing, sustainability is an operational priority.

Through long-standing partnerships with certified regional recycling centers, we divert more than 90% of all tear-off debris away from landfills:

  • Asphalt Shingles: Transported to regional processors where they are ground and recycled into recycled asphalt pavement (RAP) for Oregon highways.
  • Metals & Nails: Valley metals, lead flashings, and roofing nails are sorted via electromagnetic separators for metal smelting recovery.
  • Wood Decking & Shake: Untreated wood scrap is chipped and converted into clean industrial biomass and landscaping mulch.

Industry Accreditations & Licensing

Cascade Roofing holds Oregon Construction Contractors Board license #36149. We carry $2,000,000 in comprehensive general liability coverage and full statutory workers' compensation insurance on every crew member.

As a CertainTeed Select ShingleMaster™, we are among the top 1% of roofing contractors in North America qualified to furnish the manufacturer’s 50-year non-prorated SureStart PLUS warranty.
